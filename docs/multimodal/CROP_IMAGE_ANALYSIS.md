# Crop and leaf observations

Supported appearance labels: discoloration, yellowing, brown spots, leaf holes,
wilting appearance, mold-like appearance, visible insects, physical/stem damage,
unusual leaf shape, visible droplets and apparent stress. No visible damage in a
limited photo is not proof that a plant is healthy.

Visible observations are shown before possible explanations. Uncertain features
and inconclusive images cannot generate possible causes. A mismatch between
selected and detected image kinds returns `image_kind_mismatch`. There is no
image-only species identification; a farmer-entered crop remains provisional and
may be wrong. Wrong crop/region or a feature outside the small vetted collection
returns no matching evidence, not a generic model diagnosis.

The initial health collection supports maize yellowing/striping context and soil
surface assessment only. Other labels can be observed but often lack explanatory
references. Iowa and Minnesota sources are labeled with their original geography,
not silently calibrated for Myanmar. Exact region filters do not treat unknown or
US scope as a Myanmar match. Obtain qualified local extension review.

A single photo cannot distinguish nutrient, pest, fungal, bacterial, viral,
drought, waterlogging, aging or mechanical causes. No disease probability,
pesticide choice, fertilizer dose, crop suitability, ranking or official weather
warning is generated. Follow-ups request clearer views, symptom distribution,
growth stage, dated weather/management history and expert review.
