================================================================================
FIELD SHIFT - ADAPTING FARMS WITH NASA DATA
Farm-decision data sources: factor -> API -> what it returns -> sample
================================================================================
Compiled 2026-11-14 for the Northeast Bay / NASA Space Apps 2026 challenge.
Everything marked [WORKS] was called from the team laptop today and the result
is stored in data-pack/csv/. Nothing here is invented: a source that needs a
login is labelled, and a factor with no honest source is labelled [GAP].

HOW TO READ THE TAGS
  [WORKS]      public, no key, called today, output in data-pack/csv/
  [KEY]        free, but you must create an account and get a key/token
  [LOGIN]      Earthdata Login (free) + earthaccess; granule download, not a
               simple JSON call
  [US ONLY]    the dataset or API covers the United States only
  [NO API]     bulk download or manual entry only
  [GAP]        no defensible data source. Show "not known" in the UI.
  [FAILED]     endpoint did not answer from our network today

WHY THE SPLIT MATTERS FOR OUR PILOT
  Our three fields are in Myanmar (Hlegu 16.87N 96.20E, Bago 17.33N 96.48E,
  Magway 20.15N 94.93E). USDA SSURGO, CDL, NASS and PRISM are US-only, so they
  CANNOT be the backbone of our demo. Global sources carry the demo:
  NASA POWER + MODIS + GIBS + ISRIC SoilGrids + Open-Meteo. The US-only paths
  are documented and demonstrated on a Nevada reference point because the
  challenge statement itself uses a Nevada alfalfa farm.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0. THE SHORT ANSWER - WHAT TO PUT IN THE APP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FACTOR                     PRIMARY SOURCE                    CADENCE / GRID
1 Weather & climate        NASA POWER daily + monthly        1 day / 0.5 deg
                           Open-Meteo 16-day forecast       1 day / ~9 km
2 Soil condition           ISRIC SoilGrids (pH, texture,     250 m / 6 depths
                           SOC, N, CEC, Ksat)
                           NASA POWER GWETTOP/GWETROOT      1 day / 0.5 deg
                           SMAP L3/L4 (optional, needs login) 9 km / daily
3 NASA Earth observation   MODIS NDVI/EVI, LST, land cover   250 m-1 km
                           GIBS map tiles (no key)           pre-rendered
4 Crop information         Farmer input + our crop library   n/a
                           + NASA POWER climate suitability  monthly
5 Crop health              MODIS NDVI anomaly + LST anomaly  weekly
                           + farmer photo (our own model)    on demand
6 Farm management          Farmer input (local storage)      n/a
                           + GloFAS river discharge          1 day

The honest one-liner: NASA tells us what the weather, soil and vegetation did;
the farmer tells us what he did; our engine combines the two and says which
rotation is defensible. We never claim a satellite measured pH or disease.

1. FACTOR 1 - WEATHER AND CLIMATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Need: current temperature, daily min/max, weekly forecast, rainfall amount and
probability, humidity, wind speed/direction, solar radiation, drought and
heavy-rain signals.

1.1 NASA POWER - daily point time series                        [WORKS]
    https://power.larc.nasa.gov/api/temporal/daily/point
      ?parameters=T2M,T2M_MAX,T2M_MIN,PRECTOTCORR,RH2M,WS2M,WD2M,
                  ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN,GWETTOP,GWETROOT
      &community=AG&latitude=16.87&longitude=96.20
      &start=20260715&end=20261112&format=CSV
    Auth: none. One request per point. CSV or JSON.
    Returns: one row per day. Columns and units:
      T2M                C          mean temperature at 2 m
      T2M_MAX / T2M_MIN  C          daily max / min
      PRECTOTCORR        mm/day     corrected precipitation
      RH2M               %          relative humidity
      WS2M               m/s        wind speed
      WD2M               deg        wind direction
      ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN  MJ/m2/day  solar radiation
      GWETTOP            fraction   surface soil wetness (0-1)
      GWETROOT           fraction   root-zone soil wetness (0-1)
    Sample (data-pack/csv/power_daily_hlegu.csv):
      date,t2m_c,t2m_max_c,t2m_min_c,prectotcorr_mm_day,rh2m_pct,...
      2026-07-15,27.0,30.24,24.38,24.37,87.87,3.19,207.6,13.98,0.97,0.99
    Limits: MERRA-2 reanalysis on a 0.5 x 0.625 deg cell (~55 x 69 km). It is
    NOT a forecast: expect a 1-2 day lag and no "rain probability" field.
    App use: seasonal water balance, GDD, heat-stress months, soil wetness.

1.2 NASA POWER - monthly means                                  [WORKS]
    https://power.larc.nasa.gov/api/temporal/monthly/point
      ?parameters=...&community=AG&latitude=...&longitude=...
      &start=2016&end=2025&format=CSV
    Returns: one row per parameter per year, JAN..DEC + ANN. We pivot it to
    year,month,<parameter>. NOTE: PRECTOTCORR stays a mm/DAY rate here; the
    engine multiplies by days in month (this is the trap in our build_data.py).
    Sample: 2016,1,JAN,23.62,36.02,13.81,1.19,54.84,...
    App use: the 10-year normals the rotation engine already consumes.

1.3 NASA POWER - climatology (25-year normals)                   [WORKS]

2. FACTOR 2 - SOIL CONDITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Need: texture, pH, organic matter, N/P/K, moisture, drainage, fertility.
Rule we must keep: a satellite or a photo cannot measure N, P, K or pH.

2.1 ISRIC SoilGrids - global soil properties at 250 m              [WORKS]
    Point query over the public map server (WMS GetFeatureInfo):
    https://maps.isric.org/mapserv?map=/map/{PROPERTY}.map
      &SERVICE=WMS&VERSION=1.1.1&REQUEST=GetFeatureInfo
      &LAYERS={PROPERTY}_{DEPTH}_mean&QUERY_LAYERS={PROPERTY}_{DEPTH}_mean
      &STYLES=&FORMAT=image/png&INFO_FORMAT=text/html&SRS=EPSG:4326
      &BBOX={lon-0.25},{lat-0.25},{lon+0.25},{lat+0.25}
      &WIDTH=101&HEIGHT=101&X=50&Y=50
    PROPERTY: phh2o, clay, sand, silt, soc, nitrogen, cec, bdod, cfvo
    DEPTH:    0-5cm, 5-15cm, 15-30cm, 30-60cm, 60-100cm, 100-200cm
    Returns: an HTML fragment with Value + Unit (the WMS does not serve JSON).
    Sample (real answer at Hlegu, 0-5 cm):
      phh2o   Value 63  | Unit pH*10      -> pH 6.3
      clay    Value 334 | Unit g/kg       -> 33.4 % clay
      soc     Value 429 | Unit dg/kg      -> 42.9 g/kg
      nitrogen Value 274 | Unit cg/kg     -> 2.74 g/kg
    Units to respect: phh2o is pH x 10; clay/sand/silt are g/kg (divide by 10
    for %); soc is dg/kg; nitrogen is cg/kg; cec is mmol(c)/kg; bdod is cg/cm3.
    Every CSV row keeps BOTH: value/unit exactly as the provider sent it, and
    value_si/unit_si already converted (pH 6.3, clay 33.4 %). The app should
    read value_si; value/unit stays so a reviewer can check us against the API.
    NOTE: ksat is advertised in the SoilGrids docs but the /map/ksat.map layer
    does not answer, so we collect bulk density (bdod) and derive a drainage
    class from clay % + bulk density instead of pretending to have Ksat.
    NOTE: the nicer REST API rest.isric.org timed out from our network today;
    the map server above answered. Both are the same 250 m product.
    App use: texture class, drainage proxy (clay + Ksat), acidity flag, SOC.
    This is the replacement for SSURGO outside the US - it covers Myanmar.

2.2 USDA SSURGO / STATSGO2 via Soil Data Access                  [WORKS][US ONLY]
    POST https://sdmdataaccess.sc.egov.usda.gov/Tabular/post.rest
    Body: {"query": "<SQL>", "format": "JSON"}
    Auth: none. It is a SQL endpoint, so it returns exactly the columns asked.
    Point lookup helper: SDA_Get_Mukey_from_intersection_with_WktWgs84()
    Sample query (components at the Nevada reference point):
      SELECT TOP 10 mu.mukey, mu.muname, c.compname, c.comppct_r,
             c.drainagecl, c.hydgrp, c.taxorder, c.slope_r
      FROM mapunit mu INNER JOIN component c ON c.mukey = mu.mukey
      WHERE mu.mukey IN (SELECT mukey FROM
        SDA_Get_Mukey_from_intersection_with_WktWgs84('POINT(-118.8 39.5)'))
      ORDER BY c.comppct_r DESC
    Returns: map unit, component name, %, drainage class, hydrologic group,
    taxonomic order, slope. Horizon query (chorizon) adds clay/sand/silt,
    organic matter, pH 1:1, available water capacity.
    App use: US demo only. Do NOT call it for Myanmar - it returns no rows.

    Root zone 9 km 3-hr: short_name SPL4SMGP    (NSIDC DAAC, HDF5)

2.6 Photo-based soil assessment - our idea, kept honest            [GAP]
    There is NO public API that returns pH, N, P, K or exact moisture from a
    soil photo. Anyone claiming otherwise is guessing. A photo CAN support:
      - texture class (visual sand/silt/clay impression)  -> low confidence
      - surface colour (organic matter, iron, pale leached layer)
      - surface crusting / cracking (clay behaviour, compaction)
      - visible wetness, residue cover, erosion rills
      - stones / coarse fragments
    Recommended 5-photo protocol (farmer takes all 5, same visit):
      1 soil surface, 2 handful broken open (texture), 3 colour next to a
      white reference (paper/coin), 4 wider view of the bed, 5 a 30 cm hole
      profile with a ruler in frame for scale.
    Output must be a CLASS + CONFIDENCE, never a number: e.g.
      "looks clay-rich (medium confidence) - matches SoilGrids 39% clay".
    The photo's real job is to CONFIRM or CONTRADICT SoilGrids/POWER at that
    point. When they disagree, show both and ask for a lab test.
    Fusion rule for the app:
      soil_value = SoilGrids (base) + POWER wetness (state)
                   + photo (confidence modifier, never replaces the base)
    If we want chemistry: a $10 soil test kit or a lab. Add those as manual
    fields in the same form, with a "measured by" date.

2.7 Soil test kit / lab result                                    [NO API]
    Manual entry: pH, N-P-K (ppm), organic matter %, lab name, sample date.
    This is the only route to real chemistry and it must outrank any estimate.

3. FACTOR 3 - NASA EARTH OBSERVATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Need: vegetation health (NDVI), land surface temperature, soil moisture,
rainfall, vegetation change, land cover, drought indicators.

3.1 MODIS NDVI / EVI - 250 m, 16-day (point API)                   [WORKS]
    https://modis.ornl.gov/rst/api/v1/MOD13Q1/subset
      ?latitude=16.87&longitude=96.20
      &startDate=A2024001&endDate=A2025365
      &band=250m_16_days_NDVI&kmAboveBelow=0&kmLeftRight=0
    Auth: none (ORNL DAAC MODIS/VIIRS web service, one pixel, no image).
    Returns JSON: every 16-day composite with a raw integer and a scale.
    Sample (Hlegu, 2025-01-01): data 4981, scale 0.0001 -> NDVI 0.4981
    Band names: 250m_16_days_NDVI and 250m_16_days_EVI.
    LIMIT WE HIT: one call may cover at most ~10 composites, so a 2-year
    request returns HTTP 400 "exceeds maximum subset tiles support of 10".
    Our collector splits the range into 140-day chunks and merges them.
    App use: crop vigour curve, seasonal green-up, anomaly vs the same
    fortnight in previous years. This is the "vegetation health" factor.

3.2 MODIS land surface temperature - 1 km, 8-day                    [WORKS]
    Same service, product MOD11A2, bands LST_Day_1km / LST_Night_1km.
    Raw integer, scale 0.02, unit Kelvin -> we convert to C in the CSV.
    Raw integer, scale 0.02, unit Kelvin; our CSV converts to C.
    Sample (Hlegu 2024-2025): day mean 32.4 C (27.4-39.5), night mean 24.8 C.
    COVERAGE TRAP: 41 of 92 day composites came back as raw 0 at this pixel.
    MODIS uses 0/65535 as "not produced", so a naive conversion shows -273 C.
    Our collector blanks them; treat a gap as unknown, never as cold.
    App use: heat stress on the canopy surface (not air temperature), and
    the night value as a proxy for heat retention / crop respiration stress.

3.3 MODIS land cover - 500 m, yearly                                [WORKS]
    Product MCD12Q1, band LC_Type1, startDate=A2015001&endDate=A2024365.
    Returns IGBP class codes (12 = cropland, 14 = cropland/natural mosaic).
    App use: confirm the pixel really is cropland; context for the field.

3.4 VIIRS NDVI/EVI - 500 m, 16-day                        [AVAILABLE][LOGIN]
    Product VNP13A1, band 500m_16_days_NDVI. Higher revisit than MODIS.
    CMR confirms granules over our bbox (latest example A2025361, 2025-12-27),
    but the point-value pull was not run here (LP DAAC/AppEEARS login).
    Use it when MODIS has a cloudy gap; same anomaly method.

3.5 Landsat 8/9 Collection 2 Level-2 - 30 m                    [LOGIN/USGS]
    Search: CMR short_name LANDSAT_8_C2_L2 / LANDSAT_9_C2_L2
            (confirm the exact short_name with
             https://cmr.earthdata.nasa.gov/search/collections.json?keyword=Landsat)
    Access: USGS M2M API (token) or earthaccess/Planetary Computer.
    Bands: red, NIR, SWIR -> NDVI, NDMI, bare-soil mapping at 30 m.

3.7 GEDI L4A biomass                                            [LOGIN]
    short_name GEDI04_A (ORNL DAAC), 25 m footprints, sparse sampling.

    CMR returned no granule for our field bbox in 2025 - expected for GEDI's
    sampling pattern over a 0.8 x 0.8 degree box.

3.8 NASA GIBS - pre-rendered map tiles (no key)                    [WORKS]
    https://gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov/wmts/epsg3857/best/{LAYER}/default/
      {DATE}/GoogleMapsCompatible_Level9/{z}/{y}/{x}.jpg
    Layers we use: VIIRS_SNPP_NDVI_8Day, IMERG_Precipitation_Rate,
    MODIS_Terra_L3_Land_Surface_Temp_8Day_Day,
    MODIS_Terra_CorrectedReflectance_TrueColor.

    Sample: tiles saved in data-pack/tiles/ (~20-60 KB each).
    App use: instant map background for the Flutter field screen, no GDAL.

4. FACTOR 4 - CROP INFORMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Need: crop type, planting date, growth stage, harvest date, water requirement,
temperature range, nutrients, cycle length.

4.1 Farmer input + our crop library - the real source here           [REQUIRED]
    There is no global API that says "this field is planted with X on date Y".
    Crop type, planting date, irrigation type and drainage come from the
    farmer; agronomic limits come from our curated crop-profiles.v1.json
    (FAO/IRRI/DOA sources, currently flagged unverified until the data owner
    finishes the sheet).
    App use: the input screen. Two questions replace a dozen datasets:
    "what is growing now?" and "when did you sow it?".
    REALITY CHECK from our own data: MCD12Q1 at the Hlegu point says savanna
    (2016-2019) and grassland (2020-2024), not cropland. A 500 m land-cover
    pixel cannot see our field, so land cover must never be used to infer the
    crop. It is context ("this area is a mix of grass and farmland"), nothing
    more.

4.2 USDA Cropland Data Layer (CDL) / CropScape              [US ONLY][FAILED]
    https://nassgeodata.gmu.edu/axis2/services/CDLService/GetCDLValue
      ?year=2024&x={lon}&y={lat}
    Returns the crop class at a 30 m pixel (e.g. 36 = alfalfa).
    Status 2026-11-14: the CropScape host did not answer from our network
    (connection timeout, three attempts). The service itself is documented;
    retry from the US or use the CDL GeoTIFF download.
    App use: US demo only. It answers "what was grown here last year", which
    is exactly the rotation baseline the challenge asks about.

4.3 USDA NASS QuickStats                                     [KEY][US ONLY]
    https://quickstats.nass.usda.gov/api/api_GET/?key={KEY}&commodity_desc=CORN
      &year=2023&format=JSON
    Status today: HTTP 401 without a key (as expected).
    Returns county yields, planted acreage, production history.
    App use: calibrate "is this yield normal for this county" in the US demo.

4.4 FAO GAEZ v4 - agro-ecological suitability                  [NO API]
    https://gaez.fao.org/  - bulk layers (crop suitability, yield potential,
    soil/water constraints). Download once, join by grid cell.
    App use: independently check whether a proposed rotation crop is
    agro-climatically plausible in Myanmar.

4.5 FAOSTAT - national production and prices                    [KEY-ish]
    https://faostatservices.fao.org/api/v1/en/data/QCL?area=28&item=27
      &element=5419&year=2021,2022,2023
    Status today: DNS did not resolve from our network. The service is public

4.6 Market viability                                            [KEY / NO API]
    USDA AMS Market News API (key) for US prices;
    World Bank Pink Sheet (monthly xlsx) for global commodity prices;
    national/wholesale price lists for Myanmar.
    App use: "is there a buyer for this rotation crop?" - a farmer constraint

5. FACTOR 5 - CROP HEALTH AND DISEASE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Need: leaf colour, yellowing, spots, pest damage, fungal disease, growth
abnormality.

5.1 NDVI anomaly - vegetation stress detector                     [WORKS]
    Method (no new API): take MOD13Q1 NDVI for the field, compare each
    16-day composite to the same composite window averaged over 2024-2025.
    anomaly = (NDVI_now - NDVI_normal) / NDVI_normal
    App message: "vegetation is 18% below its normal for this fortnight".
    What it means: water stress, heat, nutrient deficiency, pest damage, or
    harvest - the index cannot tell which. Never say "disease detected".

5.2 LST anomaly - heat stress detector                            [WORKS]
    Same method on MOD11A2 LST_Day_1km. A hot canopy with normal NDVI points
    to early heat/water stress; a hot canopy with low NDVI points to bare soil.

5.3 Farmer photo diagnosis - our own model                        [GAP]
    No NASA product detects disease and no free API returns a diagnosis.
    Plan: 1-3 leaf photos -> our own classifier -> "possible nutrient
    deficiency / possible leaf spot / unclear", with confidence and a
    recommendation to confirm with the local extension officer.
    Never output a pesticide dose from a photo.

5.4 NASA FIRMS - fire and burning                                  [KEY]
    https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/api/area/  (free MAP_KEY)
    Use: smoke/burning near the field, residue burning detection in the
    rotation story. Not essential for the MVP.

5.5 SMAP / IMERG drought context                                  [LOGIN]
    Combine root-zone wetness (SPL4SMGP) with 30-day IMERG rainfall deficit
    to label "dry spell", "wet spell". Keep it a label, not a probability.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. FACTOR 6 - FARM MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Need: irrigation history, fertiliser, pesticide, planting schedule, previous
yields, expenses, water availability.

6.1 Everything in this factor is farmer-entered                    [REQUIRED]
    None of it exists in a satellite. Store locally (SQLite/SharedPreferences)
    with a date stamp. The engine already reads irrigation and drainage enums;
    the rest is for the "what we assumed" panel and future scoring.

6.2 Water availability                                          [WORKS/LOGIN]
    GloFAS river discharge (Open-Meteo flood, works) for a regional water
    signal; USGS Water Data OGC API for US gauges (works, US only):
      https://api.waterdata.usgs.gov/ogcapi/v0/collections
    Groundwater: GRACE-FO at ~300 km monthly - trend slide only, never a
    field number.

6.3 Expenses and yield history                                     [NO API]
    Manual. If the farmer does not know, leave null - the engine must not
    invent a baseline (that is the MISSING_BASELINE rule already implemented).

8. THE SIX THINGS WE MUST SHOW AS "NOT KNOWN"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Soil pH / N / P / K unless the farmer enters a lab or kit result.
2. Soil texture beyond a 250 m prior + a photo-based class with confidence.
3. Crop type and planting date unless the farmer tells us.
4. Disease identity from a leaf photo - only "possible" categories.
5. Field-level soil moisture: SMAP is 9 km, POWER is ~55 km.
6. Any yield, profit or success percentage - no data supports it.

If the UI hides these, the project becomes another app that guesses. The
engine already refuses to guess; the data pack must not undercut that.
